For designers and creative technologists

Show what your work builds on.

Nool is a process log for makers. Document a project while you make it, keep the works it draws on, and post it as a case study with its lineage. Nool is Tamil for thread, and also for book.

Download Nool Free · macOS, Windows, Linux

Every project is a stamp

Mint while you work, postmarked when you post it. Documenting is part of making, not a chore at the end.

01

The desk

Stages you name yourself. Type and press Enter for a note, paste a link, drop an image. Checklists, quotes, palettes and a book-spread moodboard.

02

Precedents

Keep the artworks, papers and code each stage draws on, with a line on why. They become the lineage of your work.

03

The letter

Post a project and it becomes an editorial case study: stages as chapters, figures, and the lineage in the margin.

04

Figma, straight in

The Send to Nool plugin sends selected frames to the stage you're working on, in one click.

05

Claude and MCP apps

A built-in MCP server lets Claude and other AI apps add notes, links and images to your projects.

06

Scripts

A small local inbox API, so a Blender script or a phone shortcut can send renders and notes too.

Your work stays yours

There is no Nool server and no account. Nool runs on your computer and your projects live in a folder on it.

  • Local firstProjects, images and notes are stored on your computer, not in the cloud.
  • Back up your waySave a single backup file, or back up to your own Google Drive. Nool can only see the backups it creates.
  • AI connects, people makeAI never generates the work. It only helps you document and find what your work is in conversation with.
